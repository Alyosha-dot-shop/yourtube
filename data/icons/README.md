# YourTube icons

The YourTube mark takes the familiar rounded "play button" screen and gives it a roof and a chimney: the tube lives in **your** house. Your watch history, subscriptions and data stay at home on your computer, with no account and no company in the middle. In the wordmark, "Your" is set in the house colour and "Tube" in ink, so the one changed word does the talking.

Colours: hearth coral `#FF6B4F` → `#EC4A36` (face gradient), chimney `#D63F2F`, base `#B5352B`, play triangle cream `#FFF7EE`, ink `#241F31` (light backgrounds) or `#FFFFFF` (dark backgrounds, `yourtube-wordmark-dark.svg`). The symbolic icon uses GNOME's `#2e3436` so GTK can recolour it. The wordmark type is Adwaita Sans Bold (SIL OFL), converted to outlines.

This mark is a homage to the play-button idea. YourTube is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to YouTube or Google.
